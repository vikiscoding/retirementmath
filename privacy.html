Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-28. A lawyer or accountant read the earlier version of this page on 2026-10-25.

This policy is for Retirement Math at retirementmath.ca, run by Vik in Toronto, Ontario.

What we collect

Nothing you type into the calculators. All math runs in your browser. Your income and age are never sent to us or stored.

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or ad scripts. After a result, the page shows a contribution link. Nothing you type is sent with that link. The calculators do not show tax software or robo-advisor links right now.

If you use the contribution link, your card details go to Stripe, not to this site.

If that changes

If we add ads (for example Google AdSense) or analytics, we will update this page first, name each provider, and ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ad Settings.

If we add an email list, we will only email you if you sign up, every email will have an unsubscribe link, and we will follow Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

Your rights

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, for Quebec residents, Law 25. You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

Contact

Privacy officer: Vik, Toronto, Ontario. Email: privacy@retirementmath.ca.
